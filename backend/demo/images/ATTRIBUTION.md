## AI-generated nail pictures

The nail pictures (French tips, red, black tips, pink tips, rose tips, and the blurry dark one) were generated by the
project author with Google's AI image generator, as variations of one picture with different nail colours. They are **not**
photos of any real person's hands or of any real salon's work. They stand in for a seller's listing photo and a
customer's photo so the dispute check can be shown on the same hand with different results.
